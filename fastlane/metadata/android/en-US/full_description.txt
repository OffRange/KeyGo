<p><i>Password Manager</i> secure, open-source Android password manager that uses AES encryption to securely store passwords and credit card information locally on the device.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Store Passwords and Credit Card information<li>Generate secure Passwords and Passphrases</li><li>Estimate the Password Strength with <a href='https://github.com/GoSimpleLLC/nbvcxz' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>nbvcxz</a></li><li>AES-Encryption on your local device</li><li>Autofill feature for easy input</li><li>Supports Material 3 and Dynamic Color (Android 12+ required)</li></ul>
